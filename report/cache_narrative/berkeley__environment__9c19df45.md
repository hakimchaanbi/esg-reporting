### Data Limitations and Omissions

Reporting under this section reflects specific boundary conditions and framework mappings. Energy metrics under Disclosure 302-1 are collected in MWh rather than Joules, and water volumes under Disclosure 303-3 are collected in cubic metres rather than megalitres, requiring conversion for standard GRI presentations. Water withdrawal metrics represent off-site third-party supply, and qualitative risk indicators under Disclosure 303-1 cover the main campus only without detailing discharge quality parameters (GRI 303-2).

Greenhouse gas baselines combine Scope 1 and Scope 2 figures into a single total rather than reporting them separately for Disclosure 305-1 and Disclosure 305-2, and specific emission factor sources and consolidation boundaries are not reported. Absolute GHG reductions under Disclosure 305-5 are derived across the combined scope boundary.

Waste figures under Disclosure 306-3, Disclosure 306-4, and Disclosure 306-5 do not include hazardous waste tonnages, as hazardous streams are managed via program protocols rather than mass tracking. Landfill and incineration streams are reported as a single combined disposal total. Procurement data under Disclosure 308-1 tracks bid solicitations rather than the total count or percentage of new suppliers screened.

The institution does not report on GRI 301-1, GRI 301-2, or GRI 301-3 (materials used and reclaimed packaging, which do not apply to higher education services), GRI 302-2 (value chain energy), GRI 302-4 (direct energy reductions in Joules), GRI 302-5 (energy reductions of sold products), GRI 305-6 (ozone-depleting emissions), GRI 305-7 (NOx, SOx, and air emissions), GRI 306-1 (upstream and downstream waste process impacts), or GRI 308-2 (supply chain environmental impact assessments). Scope 1 process emissions, location-based Scope 2 emissions, and Scope 3 capital goods and purchased goods categories were unpopulated in the source dataset.
