### Workforce Size and Composition

The University of California, Berkeley monitors its operational workforce capacity through institutional sustainability reporting frameworks, recording {{ pre_3_full_time_equivalent_of_employees }} full-time equivalent employees.

### Data Limitations and Omissions

The reporting baseline presents specific limitations when evaluated against standard disclosure frameworks. Under GRI 2-7, organisations are required to disclose total employee headcount alongside detailed disaggregations by employment contract (permanent and temporary), employment type (full-time and part-time), gender, and region. The reported figure reflects full-time equivalence rather than an individual headcount, meaning part-time positions are aggregated into full-time units. The underlying baseline provides only a single undifferentiated metric and omits these demographic and contractual categories.

Furthermore, regarding GRI 2-8, the university does not track or disclose the total number of non-employee workers whose work is controlled by the institution, nor their contractual arrangements. Reporting is limited to direct employees and enrolled students. While institutional metrics monitor the presence of significant contracting firms, they do not collect data on the headcount of external personnel working under those agreements.
