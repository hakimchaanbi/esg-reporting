### Organisational Profile and Operational Scope

The University of California, Berkeley is a public higher education institution. In accordance with GRI 2-2, the operational boundary defined for sustainability reporting comprises facilities and locations over which the university exercises operational control. This boundary includes the main campus in the City of Berkeley, the adjacent Hill campus, the Richmond Field Station, various field stations and reserves operated by the university, and campus-owned buildings across multiple locations. Within this operational footprint, institutional features reported under GRI 2-6 include the operation of a museum.

### Reporting Governance and Timeframe

The compilation of sustainability data was coordinated by personnel within the Office of Sustainability, led by the Sustainability Data and Program Manager and a Postdoctoral Fellow. ### External Assurance and Data Limitations

No independent audits or external assurance processes were used in the preparation of this report under GRI 2-5.

Certain disclosures under the GRI Universal Standards are limited by the scope of the underlying reporting framework. For GRI 2-1, official legal names, headquarters location, and specific countries of operation are not captured in the source data. Under GRI 2-2, the available material does not state whether the reporting boundary differs from institutional financial statements, nor does it detail how minority interests are consolidated. Regarding GRI 2-3, the source data lacks a uniform reporting period across all credits, and records individual preparers without providing formal contact details, reporting frequency, or publication dates. Restatements of information under GRI 2-4 are not tracked between reporting snapshots. Finally, for GRI 2-5 and GRI 2-6, explicit external assurance details are absent due to the lack of third-party verification, while information regarding supply chains, markets served, products, and services beyond physical campus features is not reported.
