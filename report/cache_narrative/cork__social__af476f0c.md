### Occupational Health, Safety, and Wellbeing

Worker representation and health management are structured around consultation mechanisms and preventive care. The university maintains an institution-wide Safety Representative Forum, which brings together staff representatives and management. Safety representatives assist with workplace health and welfare by conducting safety tours, participating in Safety Action Teams, and reviewing dangerous occurrence and accident reports.

The institution provides physical health services, behavioural health services, reduced-cost fitness opportunities, and spiritual or contemplative activities for staff. Smoking is restricted, although a total prohibition is not in place across the entirety of the campus grounds.

### Limitations and Unreported Data

Data collected under sector-specific standards present several variances from standard disclosures:
- **GRI 202-1:** The university reports entry wage floors relative to a living wage benchmark rather than statutory minimum wage rates. The standard ratio breakdown by gender is not collected, and separate wage floors for casual staff are omitted.
- **GRI 401-2 & GRI 401-3:** Benefit eligibility is supplied as an aggregate rate without full-time versus temporary or part-time comparisons. Maternity leave policy durations are stated, but headcount metrics regarding uptake, return-to-work, and retention rates by gender are not compiled.
- **GRI 403-1, GRI 403-3, GRI 403-4, & GRI 403-6:** Occupational health data demonstrate committee existence and wellbeing programmes, but lack information on management system standards, audit boundaries, meeting frequencies, decision-making authority, or hazard identification procedures.
- **GRI 405-1:** Gender diversity metrics combine female and marginalised gender identities into a single percentage rather than distinct buckets, age group distributions are uncollected, and governance body composition percentages are not provided.

The university does not currently report on GRI 202-2 (senior management hired from the local community), GRI 204-1 (spending on local suppliers), GRI 401-1 (new hires and turnover), GRI 403-2 (hazard assessment and incident investigation), GRI 403-5 (OHS training), GRI 403-7 (OHS impacts in business relationships), GRI 403-8 (OHS management system coverage counts), GRI 403-9 (work-related injuries), GRI 403-10 (work-related ill health), or GRI 405-2 (gender remuneration ratios).
