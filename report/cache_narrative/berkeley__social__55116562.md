### Occupational Health, Safety, and Wellbeing

Workplace health and safety is coordinated through environmental health and safety structures and workplace safety committees that bring together staff and management representatives. The university provides physical health services, behavioural health services, and contemplative or spiritual activities for employees. It also offers free or reduced-cost fitness programmes and enforces a campus-wide ban on smoking and tobacco use.

Regarding standard disclosures, the university confirms joint management-worker health and safety committee structures under GRI 403-4 and health promotion services under GRI 403-6. However, detailed specifics under GRI 403-1 (legal and standard frameworks), GRI 403-3 (occupational health functions focused on hazard identification and data confidentiality), and GRI 403-4 (committee meeting frequency and decision-making authority) are not recorded.

### Reporting Limitations and Omissions

The institution does not collect data for the following standard disclosures:
- GRI 202-2: Proportion of senior management hired from the local community is not tracked.
- GRI 204-1: Spend on local suppliers is not reported; procurement tracking measures spend with social impact enterprises regardless of geographic locality.
- GRI 401-1: New employee hires and turnover rates are not reported.
- GRI 403-2, GRI 403-5, and GRI 403-7: Hazard identification processes, worker health and safety training, and management of business relationship safety impacts are not covered by the source data.
- GRI 403-8, GRI 403-9, and GRI 403-10: Occupational health management system coverage counts, work-related injury rates, and work-related ill health instances are not recorded.
- GRI 405-2: Ratio of basic salary and remuneration of women to men is not reported.
